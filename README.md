## How I approach engineering

- Connect architecture decisions to measurable business and operational needs.
- Prefer simple, explicit and maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Treat governance, security, quality and observability as architecture concerns.
- Validate important decisions through evidence, benchmarks and proofs of concept.
- Design platforms for controlled evolution rather than one-time delivery.
- Automate testing, validation, deployment and operational checks.
- Document architectural decisions, assumptions and trade-offs.
- Build reusable public references without exposing proprietary implementations.
